# WM_data_creation

This directory contains scripts for generating various wall-modeled data sets for training machine learning models. The data sets cover different flow configurations, including turbulent boundary layers, channel flow, pipe flow, and synthetic data. Generated data can be found in [here](https://github.com/yuenongling/BFM_WM/tree/master/data)

## Data Processing Utilities

* `data_processing_utils.py`:  Provides utility functions for data processing, such as importing paths and interpolating velocity profiles[cite: 2].
    * `import_path()`:  Adds necessary paths to the system for module imports.
    * `find_k_y_values()`:  Interpolates velocity values at specified locations within the boundary layer.

# CFD Simulation Case Studies

This repository contains a collection of various computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulation cases. Below is a detailed list of the cases, their descriptions, and the corresponding data sources.

### Case Descriptions

* **`Channel`**: High Reynolds number turbulent channel flow.

  * *Source:* Lozano-Duran et al. 2014, Lee et al. 2015, Hoyas et al. 2022

* **`Channel_rotate_Andersson`**: Turbulent channel flow with spanwise rotation.

  * *Source:* Andersson et al. 1995

* **`Laminar`**: Falkner-Skan laminar boundary layer (Analytical ODE + Shooting method)

* **`NACA0012_Laminar`**: Laminar flow over a NACA0012 airfoil.

  * *Source:* Swanson et al. 2016

* **`Synthetic`**: Synthetic data generated by log law (high Reynolds numbers)

* **`TBL`**: New PG-TBL cases

  * *Source:* Ling et al. 2025

* **`TBL_APG_Gasser`**: Turbulent boundary layers subjected to various pressure gradients

  * *Source:* Hirt et al. 1985

* **`TBL_Gungor`**: Turbulent boundary layers subjected to various pressure gradients

  * *Source:* Gungor et al. 2022, 2024

* **`TBL_Volino`**: Turbulent boundary layer subjected to a FPG followed by an APG

  * *Source:* Volino 2020

* **`airfoil_KTH`**: Flow over NACA 0012 and NACA 4412 airfoils at a 5° angle of attack.

  * *Source:* Tanarro et al. 2020

* **`airfoil_Tamaki`**: Flow over an A-airfoil near stall conditions.

  * *Source:* Tamaki et al. 2023

* **`apg_KTH`**: Turbulent boundary layers subjected to mild adverse pressure gradients (APG).

  * *Source:* Bobke et al. 2018 & Hosseini et al. 2016

* **`axisym_BL_Driver`**: Axis-symmetric boundary layer with separation and pressure gradient effects.

  * *Source:* Driver et al. 1991

* **`backwardstep_Driver`**: Flow over a backward-facing step, including cases with an inclined wall.

  * *Source:* Driver et al. 1985

* **`bend_Smits`**: Boundary layer flow over a convex curvature.

  * *Source:* Smits et al. 1979

* **`bump_Gaussian`**: Flow over a 2D Gaussian bump, featuring curvature and pressure gradient effects.

  * *Source:* Uzun et al. 2022

* **`conv-div_channel_Laval`**: Flow through a converging-diverging channel, leading to separation.

  * *Source:* Laval et al. 2010; Marquillie et al. 2011

* **`diffuser_Ohlsson`**: Flow through a diffuser configuration.

  * *Source:* Ohlsson et al. 2010

* **`hump_NASA_Uzun`**: Flow over the 2D NASA wall-mounted hump, a benchmark case for separation.

  * *Source:* Uzun et al. 2017

* **`periodic_hill_Balakumar`**: Flow over a series of periodic hills, a case with curvature and pressure gradients.

  * *Source:* Balakumar 2015

* **`pipe_Roma`**: High Reynolds number turbulent pipe flow.

  * *Source:* Wu et al. 2010 / Pirozzoli et al. 2020

* **`round_step_DLR`**: Flow over the 2D DLR rounded step, featuring small curvature and a pressure gradient.

  * *Source:* Alaya et al. 2021

* **`sep_bubble_Coleman`**: A pressure-induced separation bubble.

  * *Source:* Coleman et al. 2018

* **`sep_bubble_Coleman_swept`**: A separation bubble with sweep, introducing 3D flow effects.

  * *Source:* Coleman et al. 2019

* **`sep_bubble_Kamogawa`**: A pressure-induced separation bubble.

  * *Source:* Kamogawa et al. 2023

* **`smooth_ramp_Uzun`**: Flow over a smooth ramp with curvature and pressure gradient.

  * *Source:* Simmons et al. 2019; Uzun et al. 2024

* **`spinning_Driver`**: A 3D boundary layer generated by a spinning body.

  * *Source:* Driver et al. 1990

* **`strained_TBL`**: Laterally strained boundary layers with pressure gradient and 3D effects.

  * *Source:* Pompeo et al. 1993

* **`swept_wing`**: Flow over a swept wing configuration.

  * *Source:* Van Den Berg et al. 1975

* **`transition_Coupland`**: A transitional boundary layer, capturing the evolution from laminar to turbulent flow.

  * *Source:* Coupland; Roach et al. 1990
